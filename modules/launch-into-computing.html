MODULE OVERVIEW

My Learning Journey

Launch into Computing provides a broad foundation for postgraduate study in computing. The module introduces fundamental computing concepts and examines their application within modern organisations and digital environments.

The module provides an opportunity to develop a broader understanding of computing beyond my existing professional experience in fibre-optic telecommunications and education.

In particular, I am using this module to establish a stronger foundation for subsequent study in cybersecurity, network security, risk management and cybersecurity research.

04 • REFLECTIVE PRACTICE

What Have I Learnt and How?

This module has provided an important foundation for my transition into advanced cybersecurity study. My previous professional experience has given me practical exposure to telecommunications and technical infrastructure, but the module has helped me place that experience within a broader computing context.

One of the most important areas of development has been my understanding that cybersecurity cannot be considered independently from the wider computing environment. Security is influenced by software, hardware, networks, data, users, organisational processes and emerging technologies.

The module has also encouraged me to consider risk more systematically. Rather than viewing a technical problem only from an operational perspective, I am beginning to consider assets, threats, vulnerabilities, consequences and organisational requirements.

My professional experience as a Fibre Optic Engineer has made these concepts particularly relevant. I can now begin to connect physical telecommunications infrastructure with wider questions concerning network security, availability, confidentiality, integrity and resilience.

Another area of development has been my academic approach to learning. I am becoming more conscious of the need to evaluate sources critically, distinguish between evidence and opinion, and use academic literature to support conclusions.

How I Will Apply This Learning

I intend to build on this foundation in subsequent modules, particularly Network Security and Security and Risk Management. I also intend to explore how cybersecurity principles can be applied to fibre-based last-mile telecommunications infrastructure as my research interests develop.
