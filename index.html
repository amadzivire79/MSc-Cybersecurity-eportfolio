My MSc Cybersecurity Journey

I am undertaking an MSc in Cybersecurity at the University of Essex, developing advanced knowledge and practical skills across cybersecurity, networking, risk management, programming, digital forensics, cloud technologies and research.

This e-portfolio provides a structured record of my learning and professional development. It contains evidence of my academic work, reflections, feedback, meeting notes, skills development and action plans.

Cybersecurity Research

CURRENT RESEARCH INTEREST

Last-Mile Network Security

My developing research interests include the security of telecommunications infrastructure and last-mile networks, with particular interest in the security challenges associated with fibre-to-the-premises environments.

I am interested in exploring how technical controls, human factors, network architecture, governance and risk management can contribute to improving security across last-mile environments.
