01 · Professional Profile & Career Direction

A transition from telecommunications field engineering into cybersecurity, combining hands-on knowledge of physical network infrastructure with growing technical and analytical security capabilities.

Current professional foundation Established experience

  • UK FTTP installation in overhead and underground environments
  • Optical connectivity testing, fault investigation and maintenance
  • Customer-premises router and Wi-Fi troubleshooting
  • Field documentation, customer communication and practical problem-solving
  • Transferable experience in teaching, mentoring, analytical reporting and ecological fieldwork

Cybersecurity career direction Developing

  • Entry roles in security operations (SOC), network security or junior cybersecurity analysis
  • Practical proficiency in network assessment, security monitoring and incident investigation
  • Secure Python development and evidence-based risk management
  • Longer-term progression towards specialist or senior technical responsibility
  • Career ambition: work towards a salary above £70,000 within five years, dependent on experience and opportunities
Professional positioning: FTTP engineering provides insight into last-mile connectivity, customer equipment and network-edge risks. MSc study adds the methods needed to assess and communicate security risks systematically.
